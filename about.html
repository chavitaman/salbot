How it started

We wanted to eat healthy. We kept ordering DoorDash.

My girlfriend and I both love good food, and we both wanted to eat better. But by 6pm, after a full day of decisions, "what's for dinner" was one decision too many. So DoorDash won. Again.

It got real when we started training for a half marathon. What we ate actually mattered now, and winging it every night wasn't cutting it.

So I built something for us. A bot we could text, that knew our goals, remembered the recipes we saved, and had the grocery cart ready before we had to think about it.

Then friends and family started texting it too. That's salbot today: made in Chicago, shaped by everyone who texts it.
