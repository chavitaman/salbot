How the Instacart part works

salbot builds it. You check out.

No new store and no new account. salbot works with the Instacart you already use, with Kroger and Postmates coming soon.

01

salbot builds your list

From your meal plan, your saved recipes, and what's on sale at your store, with your preferences baked in.

02

You get one link

A single Instacart link lands in your texts. Tap it from anywhere.

03

Instacart opens with your items

Pick your usual store. Everything on the list is matched to real products. Swap anything you want.

04

You check out

With your own Instacart account, address, and payment. Delivery or pickup, your call.

Do I need Instacart to save recipes?

No. Saving recipes, your recipe book, and meal planning all work without any grocery account. You only need Instacart when you want to check out a cart.

Do I need an Instacart account?

Only to check out. A free Instacart account works, Instacart+ is optional, and you can shop any store Instacart carries near you. Direct integrations with Kroger and your local grocery store are on the way.

Does salbot cost money?

No. salbot is free to use. When you check out a cart through Instacart or one of our other partners, we may earn a commission through their affiliate program. You never pay extra for it.

Does salbot see my payment info?

No. You pay Instacart directly. salbot never sees your card or your Instacart password.

Can salbot check out for me?

No. salbot builds the cart, and you always review it and check out yourself.
