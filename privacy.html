Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("salbot," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website at sbt.recipes and our text message service (the "Service"). Our Terms of Use also apply.

The short version: we use what you text us to plan your meals and build your grocery carts. We never sell your personal information. We never share your mobile number or text opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

1. What we collect

  • Contact info: your mobile number, and your name if you share it.
  • Your messages: the texts, links, and photos you send salbot, and salbot's replies.
  • Food preferences: things you tell us, such as dietary preferences, foods you avoid, allergies, household size, budget, nutrition goals, and favorite ingredients.
  • Recipes and plans: recipes you save, meal plans, grocery lists, and carts salbot builds for you.
  • Store connections: if you connect a grocery account (for example Kroger), the account tokens needed to add items to your cart. We do not receive or store your payment card details; you pay the retailer directly.
  • Usage and device data: basic information from our website and message systems, such as timestamps, delivery status, browser type, and pages visited.

Some details you choose to share, like allergies or health goals, can be sensitive. Share only what you're comfortable with. We use this information only to tailor your meals and carts.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Service: reply to you, save recipes, plan meals, and build grocery lists and carts.
  • To personalize suggestions based on your preferences, history, and goals.
  • To send the messages you agreed to receive, including proactive meal plans and check-ins.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To improve salbot, including reviewing conversations to fix mistakes and improve quality.
  • To follow the law and honor opt-out requests.

3. How we share it

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, such as our text messaging provider, database and hosting providers, workflow automation tools, and AI model providers that generate salbot's responses. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Retailers you choose, such as Instacart or Kroger, when salbot creates a cart or list for you. Their own privacy policies apply once you check out with them.
  • Legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of salbot, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers, if salbot is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

Text messaging: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text message opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, except the service providers that deliver our messages.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you use salbot. If you text STOP, we stop messaging you and keep only what we need to honor your opt-out and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below), and we'll delete it within 30 days unless we're required to keep it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: reply STOP at any time.
  • Access, correct, or delete: email hello@sbt.recipes with the number you use for salbot, and we'll verify the request with a text to that number.
  • Disconnect a store: email us, or revoke salbot's access from the retailer's account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know what we collect and to request deletion. We will not discriminate against you for using them.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

salbot is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the Service, contact us and we'll delete their information.

8. Cookies and analytics

Our website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics later, we'll update this policy first.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll post the new date above, and if a change is significant we'll let you know by text or on the website.

10. Contact

Questions or requests? Email hello@sbt.recipes.
